Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Mexican Train Dominoes (accessible at mexicantraindominogame.com, the "Site") collects when you visit and play our free online domino game, how that information is used, and the choices you have. The short version: we do not require an account, we do not ask for your personal data to play, and your game progress stays on your own device.

1. Who we are

The Site is operated as an independent hobby project. If you have any questions about this policy or about your data, you can reach us at ashusuyavanshi@gmail.com.

2. Information we do not collect

  • No accounts or sign-ups. You can play every game without registering, logging in, or giving us your name or email address.
  • No profile data. We do not ask for your age, gender, location, or any other personal profile information.
  • No advertising or tracking IDs. We do not sell, rent, or share personal information to advertisers, and we do not run cross-site behavioral advertising.

3. Information stored on your device

The game saves your settings and progress so you can close your browser tab and resume later. This information is stored locally in your browser's localStorage, on your own device, and is not transmitted to us. It can include:

  • Game settings such as domino set size, number of players, difficulty, and sound or theme preferences.
  • Your current game state (round, scores, and which dominoes are in your hand and on the table) and best-score records.

This storage is technically similar to a browser "cookie" but is not used for advertising, analytics, or profiling, and it never leaves your device. You can clear it at any time by using your browser's "Clear site data"/"Clear browsing data" feature for mexicantraindominogame.com. Clearing it simply resets your saved games and preferences to their defaults.

4. Service-worker cache (offline play)

The Site is an installable web app (PWA). To let you play offline, your browser stores a copy of the Site's own files (HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and images) in a local cache via a service worker. The service worker downloads nothing beyond the Site's own assets and sends nothing back to us.

5. Server-side and hosting logs

Like almost every website, our hosting provider's servers process standard technical data (such as your IP address, browser type, and requested pages) that is generated automatically whenever any website is loaded. We use this data only insofar as it is needed to deliver the Site to you, keep it secure, and diagnose problems. We do not combine it with any game data, and we do not use it to build personal profiles.

6. Information you send us

If you email us (for example with a bug report or feedback), we receive your email address and whatever you choose to include in your message. We use it only to read and reply to your question, and we do not add you to mailing lists or share your address with third parties.

7. Children's privacy

The Site is a family-friendly domino game and is intended for a general audience. Because we do not knowingly collect personal information of any kind, children can play without providing personal data. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, please contact us at ashusuyavanshi@gmail.com and we will delete it.

8. Third-party services

The Site may link to third-party websites (for example, general reference pages about the game). This Privacy Policy covers only our Site — please review the privacy policies of any external sites you visit.

9. Your rights and choices

  • Clear local data: use your browser settings to clear site data for mexicantraindominogame.com — this removes saved games and preferences instantly and irreversibly.
  • Email requests: contact us at ashusuyavanshi@gmail.com if you want to ask what, if anything, we hold relating to you, request deletion of information you sent us, or ask any other question.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if the Site changes. The "Last updated" date at the top shows when it was last revised. Continued use of the Site after an update means you accept the revised policy.

11. Contact

Questions about this policy? Email ashusuyavanshi@gmail.com. See also our related pages: Cookie Policy, Disclaimer, About us, and Contact us.
