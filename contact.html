Contact Us

We love hearing from players! Whether you found a bug, need a rule explained, or just want to tell us about your record win, getting in touch takes one email.

📧 Email: ashusuyavanshi@gmail.com
🕒 Reply time: usually within a few days
💬 Languages: English

What you can reach us about

  • Bug reports: if the game misbehaves (a domino won't play, scores look wrong, offline mode breaks), tell us what you tapped and what happened. Mention your browser and whether you were on a phone or a computer — that helps a lot.
  • Feature ideas: new game modes, other domino variations, score-sheet tweaks — your requests go to the top of the ideas list.
  • Rules questions: unsure how the engine, open trains, or covering doubles work? Check the rules page first, and if it's still unclear, we'll happily untangle it.
  • Website feedback: anything about the strategy guide, variations, or the score sheet.
  • Privacy questions: anything about our privacy policy or cookie policy — including requests about data you've sent us.
  • Everything else: just say hi — more about this project here.

When you write, it helps to include

  1. What page you were on and what you were doing;
  2. What you expected to happen versus what actually happened;
  3. Your device type (phone / tablet / computer) and browser (Chrome, Safari, Firefox…).

Please note

  • We're a small one-person project, so replies may take a few days. Every mail is read personally.
  • We never send spam and never add your email to any list — check our privacy policy for details.
