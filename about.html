About Us

Mexican Train Dominoes is a free browser game site with one simple mission: make the classic tile-laying game available to anyone, instantly, on any device — with no downloads, no accounts, and no fuss. If you grew up shuffling a double-twelve set across the kitchen table, this is that game, rebuilt to run in your pocket.

Why we built it

Mexican Train is one of the world's favorite domino games — easy to learn, endlessly replayable, and perfect for groups. But finding a good place to play it online usually means installing an app, creating an account, sitting through ads, or downloading something. We wanted better, so we built it:

  • Free, forever. Every game, rule guide, strategy article, and printable score sheet costs nothing. There is nothing to buy.
  • No sign-up. No accounts, no email walls, no personal data. Open the page and start playing.
  • Runs anywhere. The game is built phone-first, works offline once loaded, and installs to your home screen as a web app.
  • Your data stays yours. Game saves live in your browser's own storage on your device — they are never uploaded anywhere. Read our privacy policy.

What you'll find on the site

  • A complete browser game — solo against smart computer players (easy, medium, or hard) or pass-and-play for 2–4 humans sharing one device, with double-6 through double-15 domino sets, undo, hints, and automatic scorekeeping.
  • Official step-by-step rules — from setting up the engine to scoring the round.
  • Strategy guide — how to beat the computer on hard mode.
  • Game variations — popular rule twists and player-count setups.
  • A printable score sheet — for real-table games with real dominoes.

Who is behind it

The Site is an independent project run by a single game enthusiast — no studio, no publisher, no advertisers. We are not affiliated with any domino or board-game manufacturer (see our disclaimer). Feedback, bug reports, and just saying hello are all welcome through our contact page, or directly at ashusuyavanshi@gmail.com.

Our promise

The experience stays friendly and honest: family-safe content, no tracking, no data collection, and no pay-to-play. If that ever changes, you'll read about it on this site — not buried in a settings page.
