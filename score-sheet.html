How to score Mexican Train Dominoes

  • When a round ends, each player adds up the pips (dots) on the dominoes left in their hand and writes the number in that round's row.
  • The player who played their last domino scores 0 that round. If everyone was blocked, the lowest pip count scores 0.
  • After the last round, total each column — the lowest total wins.

Tip: for the classic 3-round game just use the first three rows; the engine column is a suggestion — any round that starts with the highest double works. Need the rules? Read how to play Mexican Train Dominoes, or play the free online game and we'll keep score for you.
