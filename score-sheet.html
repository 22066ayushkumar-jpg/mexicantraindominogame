How to use this Mexican Train Score Sheet

The generator does the arithmetic for you — three picks in the toolbar and a few keystrokes per round:

  1. Pick your domino set. Double-12 is the classic 13-round game; choose Double-6, 9, 12, 15 or 18 from the Domino Set Type menu and every row rebuilds to match.
  2. Pick your players. Choose 4, 6 or 8 columns — you can rename each one right in the dark header row, where it will print.
  3. Choose a cell style. Split (Pts | Total) keeps a live running total beside each score; Single Box gives one wide blank cell per round for old-school pen and paper.
  4. Score each round. When a train ends, every player types the pips left in their hand. Running totals and the mid-game subtotal update as you type — no calculator, no adding mistakes.
  5. Let the sheet pick the winner. The lowest grand total is highlighted in green and written into the The Winner Is box automatically, ties included.
  6. Print it. Press Print Score Sheet (or Ctrl+P / ⌘P): everything except the table disappears and it lands on a single US Letter page. Print blank for a handwriting sheet, or fill it in and print the finished results.

How to score Mexican Train Dominoes

  • When a round ends, each player adds up the pips (dots) on the dominoes left in their hand and types the number in that round's row. The running total and subtotal update automatically.
  • The player who played their last domino scores 0 that round. If everyone was blocked, the lowest pip count scores 0.
  • After the final round the sheet flags the lowest grand total in green and fills in the winner — lowest total wins.
