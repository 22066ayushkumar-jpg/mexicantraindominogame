Cookie Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

Good news: Mexican Train Dominoes does not use tracking, advertising, or analytics cookies. We do not set cookies that follow you around the internet, and we never show a "cookie wall" because we do not need one. The only data the Site stores is browser localStorage on your own device, which is described in detail below.

What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files that a website stores in your browser. Some are "essential" and keep a site working, some remember your preferences, and some (set by ad networks) track your browsing across many sites for advertising purposes.

Cookies set by this Site

TypeSet by this Site?What it is for

Advertising / trackingNoThe Site carries no ad networks or cross-site trackers.

What we save in localStorage instead

To let you close the tab mid-game and resume later, the game keeps a few entries in your browser's localStorage. This data:

  • is stored only on your device — it is never uploaded to us or anyone else;
  • contains only non-personal game data: settings (domino set size, number of players, difficulty), your in-progress game state, best scores, and sound/theme choices;
  • expires only when you clear it — it cannot be read by any other website, and we cannot read it either.

Service-worker cache (offline play)

As an installable web app, the Site also lets your browser cache its own files (HTML, CSS, JavaScript, images) through a service worker so the game works offline. The service worker caches only the Site's own assets and sends nothing back to us. Not a cookie, but worth mentioning so the storage picture is complete.

How to clear this data

  • Cleanest way: open your browser's site-settings for mexicantraindominogame.com and choose "Clear site data" (also called "Cookies and site data").
  • General way: any browser "Clear browsing data" option that includes cookies/site data will also remove localStorage entries.
  • Clearing restores the game to its defaults — you simply start fresh, nothing else is affected.

Changes to this policy

If we ever add services that would set cookies (we have no plans to), we will update this page and the cookie entry table above before doing so. The "Last updated" date at the top always shows the current revision.
